Privacy Policy

Short version: we collect almost nothing, store nothing about you personally and sell nothing to anyone. Here is the full detail.

What we collect

When you use the Owedco eligibility checker, your quiz answers are processed entirely in your browser. They are not sent to our servers, not stored in a database and not linked to any identity. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or any identifying information.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand general traffic patterns — page visits, device types, and similar aggregate data. This is anonymous and tells us nothing about you individually.

If Google AdSense ads are displayed, Google may use cookies to serve relevant ads. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalised ads at adssettings.google.com.

Third-party links

Owedco links to specialist tax firms as part of our referral service. When you click through to those sites, their own privacy policies apply. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of any third-party site we link to.

Children

Owedco is intended for business owners and professionals. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and the date above. We will not change our practices in ways that are less protective without notice.

Contact us

Questions about privacy? Email hello@owedco.com and we will respond within one business day.
